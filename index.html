Flight disruption early-warning model, JFK Airport

NUS-ISS team project, 5 members · Aug to Oct 2026

Can you tell, two days ahead, which flights into JFK will be delayed 15+ minutes or cancelled? We joined 25 months of U.S. Department of Transportation flight records with archived weather forecasts, loaded them into a MySQL star-schema warehouse, and compared six model families using walk-forward validation so the model never saw the future.

The final LightGBM model reached an Average Precision of 0.44 against a 0.25 baseline and held up on unseen January to June 2026 data. About one in four arrivals was disrupted, so a model that says "everything is fine" scores 75% accuracy and catches nothing, which is why we judged models on Average Precision instead.

My part: taking it live. I built and ran the AWS deployment (SageMaker, Lambda, scheduled batch scoring), built the interactive risk dashboard used in our live demo, and pulled the final report together.
